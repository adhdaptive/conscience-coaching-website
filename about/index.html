Why Conscience?

Conscience is a Community Interest Company. Its structure reflects a commitment to social justice and widening access to professional coaching and consulting.

The name also expresses the purpose of the work: coaching and consulting that can take place outdoors, partner with nature, support change and create meaningful impact.
