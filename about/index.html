About Angela

Experience, curiosity and a belief in meaningful change

Angela Topping founded Conscience Coaching and Consulting CIC to help individuals, groups and teams understand what matters and create meaningful change.

Meet Angela

Angela is an MSc-qualified coach and mentor with more than 25 years of leadership and management experience across higher education and the NHS.

She spent 19 years working as an accountant in the Faculty of Medical Sciences at Newcastle University before becoming Head of the Newcastle Joint Research Office in 2018. There, she led a multidisciplinary, multi-agency team supporting the design, development, delivery and implementation of health and care research.

The team played an important part in the COVID-19 response. When Newcastle became one of the first sites in the world to administer a COVID-19 vaccine in December 2020, Angela gained an even clearer understanding of what mattered to her: people, culture and work that creates a meaningful impact.

Leadership, coaching and advocacy

Angela joined the R&D Forum Executive Team in 2020 and was appointed Chair of the R&D Forum CIO in October 2023. Through this role, she works to ensure that the voices of research and development leaders, managers and teams are heard by funders, regulators and policy-makers.

Throughout her career, Angela has worked as a coach, mentor, ally and volunteer. Her experience includes internal coaching and mentoring, mentoring with the 1 Hour Project and The Girls’ Network, co-chairing the Newcastle University Women’s Network, working as a Mental Health First Aider and volunteering as a listener with Samaritans.

Why Conscience?

Angela’s decision to establish Conscience was shaped by the difference she had seen coaching and mentoring make, her love of the outdoors and her MSc research into women’s experiences of solution-focused coaching outdoors while working in paid health and care roles.

She brings together professional training and lived experience of working in high-pressure, hierarchical and highly governed environments. Angela is an Associate of EMCC UK and a member of its health and wellbeing special interest group. She is also a qualified personal trainer, Lowland Leader and outdoor first aider.

Conscience is a Community Interest Company. Its structure reflects Angela’s commitment to social justice, societal change and widening access to professional coaching and consulting.

What does Conscience mean?

The name describes the purpose of the CIC: Coaching and consulting services provided Outdoors, building on a firm foundation of wellbeing by partnering with Nature to Support individual, group and team development and Change.

This Impactful, evidence-based approach Enhances understanding of what matters and explores how people, groups and teams can become the best version of themselves.

Nature can help create a safe and inclusive environment. Coaching outdoors complements other support for health and wellbeing and aligns with the shift towards community-based support, prevention and a healthier workforce.

Conscience offers coaching and wellbeing support. It does not provide clinical mental-health treatment.
