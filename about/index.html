About Angela

Experience, curiosity and a belief in meaningful change

Angela Topping founded Conscience to offer person-centred coaching that helps people create change that lasts.

Meet Angela

Angela is an MSc-qualified coach and mentor with more than 25 years’ leadership and management experience across the NHS and higher education.

Her research explored the impact of coaching outdoors. She also brings experience in coaching, mentoring, wellbeing, mental-health support and social prescribing.

Angela is an Associate Member of the European Mentoring and Coaching Council, EMCC UK. Her work is person-centred, practical and grounded in what matters to each person, group or team.

Why Conscience?

Conscience is a Community Interest Company. Its structure reflects Angela’s commitment to social justice and widening access to professional coaching and consulting.

Working outdoors matters too. Nature creates a different kind of space, away from familiar routines and surroundings. It can give people room to slow down, notice what is happening and think about what comes next.

Conscience offers coaching and wellbeing support. It does not provide clinical mental-health treatment.
