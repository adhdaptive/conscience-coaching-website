Nature-based coaching

Support shaped around what matters to you

Outdoor, solution-focused coaching for individuals, groups and teams facing change, pressure or questions about what comes next.

How the coaching works

Conscience coaching takes place outdoors in a safe and supportive environment. The work starts with a free introductory chemistry session, giving you and Angela a chance to decide whether the coaching relationship feels right.

This is followed by up to six one-hour coaching sessions at an agreed outdoor location. Sessions may be one-to-one or designed for a group or team.

The approach is solution-focused. You will identify strengths, gain clarity and work towards positive actions and achievable goals, rather than staying fixed on the problem.

Non-clinical support

Coaching, not treatment

Conscience provides coaching and wellbeing support. It does not diagnose conditions or provide clinical mental-health treatment.

What sessions can explore

Work, wellbeing and change

Stress and resilience

Understand what is creating pressure and find practical ways to respond.

Confidence and self-awareness

Recognise your strengths, understand your patterns and make decisions with greater confidence.

Work and balance

Explore work-life balance, remaining in work or returning after time away.

Change and direction

Think through change at work or in everyday life, including a possible change of career or direction.

Between sessions

Follow-up reflections, resources and email support are available between sessions. These can help you hold on to useful thoughts and continue the work in everyday life.

What people report

Participants often report better mental wellbeing, more self-awareness and confidence, less stress, greater resilience and clarity, and a stronger connection with nature.
